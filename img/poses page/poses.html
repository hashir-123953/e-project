Jumping Jacks

Stand straight, jump to spread legs apart and raise arms above.

High Knees

Stand straight and lift your knees up alternately as if running.

Burpees

Start standing, squat down, place hands on the ground, jump back into a push-up position, perform a push-up, then jump back and explode up.

Mountain Climbers

In a push-up position, alternate bringing knees to the chest rapidly.

Skaters

Jump side to side, landing on one foot and bending the knee.

Butt Kickers

Run in place while kicking your heels up to your glutes.
