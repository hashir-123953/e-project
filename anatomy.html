Aerobics Poses by Anatomy

Target specific muscle groups with our categorized aerobic exercises. Learn which poses work which muscles and create balanced workout routines.

Understanding Muscle Groups

A balanced aerobic workout targets different muscle groups. Understanding anatomy helps create effective routines that strengthen your entire body while preventing imbalances and injuries.

Upper Body

Exercises that target the muscles in the arms, shoulders, chest, and back for strength and endurance. Upper body aerobics improve posture, increase upper body strength, and enhance functional fitness.

Biceps Triceps Deltoids Pectorals Trapezius Latissimus Dorsi

Recommended Poses:

  • Push-Ups Beginner
  • Tricep Dips Intermediate
  • Bicep Curls Beginner
  • Shoulder Press Intermediate

Lower Body

Exercises focused on the legs, glutes, and lower back to strengthen and tone lower body muscles. These exercises improve balance, increase leg strength, and boost overall mobility.

Quadriceps Hamstrings Glutes Calves Hip Flexors Lower Back

Recommended Poses:

  • Squats Beginner
  • Lunges Intermediate
  • Leg Raises Beginner
  • Jump Squats Advanced

Core

Core exercises engage the muscles around the abdomen, hips, and lower back for stability and strength. A strong core improves posture, reduces back pain, and enhances athletic performance.

Rectus Abdominis Obliques Transverse Abdominis Erector Spinae Hip Flexors

Recommended Poses:

  • Planks Intermediate
  • Russian Twists Intermediate
  • Mountain Climbers Advanced
  • Bicycle Crunches Intermediate

Full Body

Exercises that engage multiple muscle groups for a comprehensive workout. Full body exercises maximize calorie burn, improve coordination, and provide efficient total-body conditioning.

Multiple Groups Cardiovascular Full Integration Coordination

Recommended Poses:

  • Burpees Advanced
  • Jumping Jacks Beginner
  • High Knees Beginner
  • Mountain Climbers Advanced
